`<LinkPreview>` shows a link as a card with a title and description.

## Linking to another post

Pass the path of a post. The title and description come from its frontmatter:

```mdx
<LinkPreview url="/blog/writing-posts-in-mdx" />
```

<LinkPreview url="/blog/writing-posts-in-mdx" />

## Linking to an external page

Pass a full URL. The card uses the page's Open Graph data:

```mdx
<LinkPreview url="https://ui.shadcn.com" />
```

<Callout type="info" title="Fetched at build time">
  External pages are fetched once, when the site is built. Your readers do not
  send any requests to that page. If the page cannot be reached during the
  build, a "Page Not Found" card is shown instead.
</Callout>
